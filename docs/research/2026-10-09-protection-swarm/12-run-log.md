The coordinator adopted that amendment. The result is seven foundation investments, three gated protection prototypes, three deferrals and one rejected family of claimed security foundations. No minority plan vote was invented; actual dissent and unresolved objections remain in the decision ledger.

The final design defines shared contracts, the complete offline pipeline, staged admission, comparative attacks, one cost envelope and the stopping rules attached to the vote. No product implementation or new security-performance experiment occurred. The implementation and proof of improved resistance remain future work.
