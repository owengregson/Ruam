# Ambitious offline protection ideation

**Result: one conditional research idea, selected by all three workers.** Replace eligible application algorithms and jointly synthesize the state maintained across their events. The intended benefit is harder predictive comprehension and harder partial rule changes; neither benefit has been demonstrated, and cheap cloning remains an explicit possibility.

Read the [selected design and first falsifier](20-selected-design.md). The campaign produced 23 overlapping nominations, challenged them through actual cross-worker discussion, froze three finalists plus a select-none option, and collected one ballot per worker. The coordinator did not vote. A fourth worker could not be spawned because of the tool's concurrency limit; additional briefs were not counted as additional people.
