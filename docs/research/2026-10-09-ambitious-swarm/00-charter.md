# Ambitious offline architecture track

Requested during implementation on 2026-10-09. This track runs alongside the regional implementation; it does not delay or silently enlarge that PR's protection claims.

The user asks for more ambitious architectural ideas, with ideation, discussion, design, evaluation and argumentation, ending in **one or two ideas total**. Strictly offline, server-free JavaScript remains mandatory. The attacker has the complete artifact, compiler, seed, chosen inputs and unrestricted instrumentation. Original-source recovery, behavioral reconstruction, useful modification and comprehension are different tasks; the design must name which becomes harder. Whole-artifact copying is always possible.

The agent tool again refused a fourth worker. The coordinator develops a candidate set while three workers implement. As implementation workstreams hand off, those workers generate independent candidate sets and then cross-examine the combined pool. Roles are not additional voters. No invented discussion or measurements are allowed.

## Selection method

1. Diverge: publish independent mechanisms, concrete examples, reconstruction obstacles, cheapest defeats and costs.
2. Argue: each reviewer attempts to collapse another proposal to an old dead end or a reusable recovery algorithm.
3. Design: strengthen the best surviving mechanism's exact compilation/runtime contracts, without relying on a secret or obscuring the attack model.
4. Evaluate: distinguish inexpensive runnable attacks on existing analogues from unimplemented hypotheses. Human comprehension is measured by specific explanation/edit tasks on unseen programs, not source length or an agent's impression.
5. Select: at most two research designs. A selection funds a falsifier, not a claim of vastly improved protection. A failed idea stays rejected even if it is ambitious.

No production changes belong to this track until separately validated. The current implementation and merge request remain governed by their frozen acceptance and stopping rules.
